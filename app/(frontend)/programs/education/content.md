## Program Overview Section
**Title:** About Our Education Program
**Content:**
The Educational Support Program at the Caywood Brown Foundation is designed to address barriers to education and create pathways to success for students from underserved communities.

Through scholarships, mentoring, tutoring, and educational resources, we help students at all levels—from elementary school through college—achieve their academic goals and prepare for future success.

Our approach is holistic, addressing not only academic needs but also the social, emotional, and practical challenges that can impact educational achievement. We recognize that each student has unique needs and potential, and we tailor our support accordingly.

Since launching our education initiatives in 2007, we've supported over 3,000 students across 48 communities. Our programs have contributed to improved academic performance, higher graduation rates, and increased college attendance among participants.

## Program Components Section
**Title:** Program Components
**Description:** Our Educational Support Program includes several key components designed to provide comprehensive support to students at various stages of their educational journey.

**Components:**

1. **Scholarship Program**
   - **Description:** Financial support for higher education and vocational training
   - **Details:** Awards ranging from $500 to $5,000 based on need and merit
   - **Eligibility:** Students from underserved communities with demonstrated financial need and academic potential
   - **Application Process:** Online application with academic records, personal statement, and recommendations
   - **Support:** Renewable scholarships with academic support services

2. **Mentoring Initiative**
   - **Description:** One-on-one guidance from trained mentors
   - **Details:** Regular meetings focused on academic support, life skills, and personal development
   - **Mentor Requirements:** Professional experience, background check, and mentor training
   - **Commitment:** Minimum one-year commitment to provide consistent support
   - **Impact:** Students with mentors show improved academic performance and self-confidence

3. **Academic Tutoring**
   - **Description:** Subject-specific academic support
   - **Details:** Individual and small group tutoring in core subjects
   - **Tutors:** Certified teachers, college students, and professional volunteers
   - **Schedule:** After-school, weekend, and online options
   - **Subjects:** Math, Science, Language Arts, and Social Studies

4. **College & Career Preparation**
   - **Description:** Resources and guidance for post-secondary planning
   - **Details:** College application assistance, test preparation, career exploration
   - **Workshops:** SAT/ACT prep, college essay writing, financial aid application
   - **Resources:** College visits, career fairs, and internship opportunities
   - **Support:** First-generation college student support network

5. **Family Support Services**
   - **Description:** Resources for families to support student success
   - **Details:** Parent workshops, resource connections, and advocacy support
   - **Topics:** Understanding school systems, supporting learning at home, planning for college
   - **Approach:** Culturally responsive and accessible to diverse families
   - **Goal:** Empower families as partners in their children's education

## Impact & Outcomes Section
**Title:** Our Impact
**Description:** The Educational Support Program has made a significant difference in the lives of thousands of students.

**Impact Metrics:**
- **3,000+** students served since program inception
- **92%** high school graduation rate among program participants
- **78%** college enrollment rate (compared to 45% community average)
- **85%** first-generation college students among scholarship recipients
- **$2.5M+** in scholarships awarded

## Eligibility & Application Section
**Title:** How to Apply
**Description:** Information about eligibility criteria and application processes for our educational programs.

**Scholarship Program:**
- **Eligibility:** 
  - Resident of one of our 48 service communities
  - Demonstrated financial need
  - Minimum 2.5 GPA
  - Community involvement or service
  - Acceptance to accredited college, university, or vocational program
- **Application Requirements:**
  - Completed application form
  - Academic transcripts
  - Two recommendation letters
  - Personal statement
  - Financial information
- **Deadlines:**
  - March 15 for Fall semester
  - October 15 for Spring semester
- **Selection Process:**
  - Application review by committee
  - Finalist interviews
  - Notification within 6 weeks of deadline

**Mentoring & Tutoring Programs:**
- **Eligibility:**
  - Students grades K-12 in service communities
  - Commitment to regular participation
  - Parent/guardian permission
- **Application Process:**
  - Referral from school, community organization, or self-referral
  - Initial meeting with program coordinator
  - Assessment of needs and goals
  - Mentor/tutor matching
- **Program Duration:**
  - Academic year schedule (September-June)
  - Summer program options available

## Volunteer Section
**Title:** Volunteer Opportunities
**Description:** Make a difference in a student's life by sharing your knowledge and experience.

**Volunteer Roles:**
1. **Academic Mentor**
   - Meet regularly with assigned student(s)
   - Provide academic guidance and support
   - Serve as a positive role model
   - Commitment: 4 hours/month minimum for one year

2. **Subject Tutor**
   - Provide instruction in specific subject areas
   - Work with individuals or small groups
   - Develop customized learning activities
   - Commitment: 2-8 hours/month, flexible scheduling

3. **College & Career Coach**
   - Guide students through college application process
   - Assist with resume building and interview skills
   - Share career insights and experiences
   - Commitment: Varies by season, highest need August-January

4. **Workshop Presenter**
   - Conduct specialized workshops in your area of expertise
   - Topics may include financial literacy, specific careers, or skills
   - Materials and facilitation support provided
   - Commitment: One-time or recurring, as available

**Volunteer Requirements:**
- 21 years of age or older (18+ for college student tutors)
- Background check clearance
- Attendance at orientation and training sessions
- Relevant experience or knowledge
- Commitment to program values and guidelines

**How to Become a Volunteer:**
1. Complete volunteer application
2. Attend information session
3. Complete background check
4. Participate in training
5. Begin volunteer assignment
