## Google Partnership Section
**Title:** Google Digital Skills Training
**Description:** We've partnered with Google to provide comprehensive digital skills training to students in underserved communities. This initiative equips young people with the technical knowledge and practical skills needed for today's digital economy.

**Program Components:**
1. **Coding Fundamentals:** Introduction to programming concepts, web development, and app creation.
2. **Digital Marketing:** Search engine optimization, social media marketing, and analytics.
3. **Career Certification:** Google-recognized certifications that boost employability and career prospects.
4. **Mentorship:** One-on-one guidance from Google professionals and industry experts.
