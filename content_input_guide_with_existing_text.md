The Caywood Brown Foundation was established in 2005 by Sarah Caywood and Michael Brown, two community activists who recognized the need for comprehensive support services in underserved areas. What began as a small initiative operating out of a church basement has grown into a respected organization with national reach.

Sarah, a former educator, and Michael, a healthcare professional, combined their expertise to address the interconnected challenges facing vulnerable communities. They believed that by taking a holistic approach—focusing on education, health, and community development—they could create lasting positive change.

In the early years, the foundation relied entirely on volunteer support and small donations from community members. Despite limited resources, their passion and commitment allowed them to make a significant impact, starting with after-school tutoring programs and community health workshops.

As word spread about their effective programs, the foundation gained recognition and support from larger donors and partner organizations. This growth enabled the expansion of services to more communities and the development of more comprehensive programs.

Today, the Caywood Brown Foundation serves thousands of individuals annually across 48 communities. While we've grown considerably from our humble beginnings, we remain committed to our founding principles of compassion, integrity, and community-centered service. Every decision we make is guided by our dedication to empowering individuals and strengthening communities.
