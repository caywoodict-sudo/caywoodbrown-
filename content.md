# Caywood Brown Foundation - Content Development Plan

## Overview
This document outlines the content needed for the Caywood Brown Foundation website. The content is organized by page and section to ensure consistency across the site and alignment with the foundation's mission and goals.

## Mission Statement
The Caywood Brown Foundation is dedicated to improving lives through community-focused initiatives, educational programs, and support services that create lasting positive change. We believe in empowering individuals to reach their full potential and building stronger communities together.

## Content Strategy
- **Voice & Tone**: Compassionate, inspiring, and professional
- **Target Audience**: Potential donors, volunteers, program participants, community partners
- **Key Messaging**: Emphasize impact, community involvement, and opportunities to contribute
- **Visual Elements**: Use authentic imagery that reflects the communities served, showcases impact, and creates emotional connections

## Content Required by Page

### 1. Homepage
- Hero section with compelling headline and subheading
- Impact statistics with verifiable numbers
- Mission statement and foundation overview
- Program highlights with brief descriptions
- Testimonials from program participants or beneficiaries
- Featured partnership content (Google Digital Skills Training)
- Upcoming events with dates and brief descriptions
- Donation options with impact descriptions
- Newsletter signup content

### 2. About Page
- Organization history and founding story
- Mission, vision, and values statements
- Achievement timeline
- Organizational structure information
- Financial transparency data

### 3. Team Page
- Board of Directors with bios, photos, and credentials
- Staff members with roles and brief backgrounds
- Advisory committee information
- Volunteer leadership profiles

### 4. Programs Pages
- **Education**
  - Program overview and objectives
  - Details on scholarships, mentoring, and educational resources
  - Success stories and outcomes
  - Application information

- **Health**
  - Program description and goals
  - Health services provided
  - Community health initiatives
  - Impact statistics and testimonials

- **Community Development**
  - Program scope and objectives
  - Community initiatives description
  - Partnership opportunities
  - Success stories

- **Google Training**
  - Partnership details
  - Program components (coding, digital marketing, etc.)
  - Certification information
  - Application process

### 5. Events Pages
- Upcoming events with details (date, time, location, description)
- Past events archive with outcomes and photos
- Event hosting information
- Registration details

### 6. Blog Pages
- Regular articles on foundation activities
- Impact stories
- Educational content related to the foundation's mission
- News and updates
- Archive of past posts
- Categories for easy navigation

### 7. Donate Page
- Donation options and levels
- Impact of different donation amounts
- Monthly giving program details
- Corporate sponsorship information
- Other ways to give (in-kind, planned giving)

### 8. Volunteer Page
- Volunteer opportunities and roles
- Requirements and expectations
- Application process
- Volunteer testimonials and stories

### 9. Fundraise Page
- Ways to fundraise for the foundation
- Fundraising event ideas and guidelines
- Support provided by the foundation
- Success stories

### 10. Partner Page
- Current partners showcase
- Partnership opportunities
- Benefits of partnering
- Application process

## Content Development Timeline
1. Gather existing content and assess gaps
2. Develop core messaging and key content sections
3. Create first draft of priority pages (Home, About, Programs)
4. Develop supporting content (Blog, Events, etc.)
5. Review and refine all content
6. Implement content in website design
7. Regular content updates and maintenance

## Content Management
- Responsibility for content updates
- Review process for new content
- Content calendar for blog posts and updates
- Guidelines for image selection and optimization
